Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: {{ lastUpdated }}

📋 Phase One Notice: Evenzoo does not currently process payments on the Platform. This policy will take effect once in-platform payments are introduced.

1. Current Phase

Evenzoo is currently in an early testing phase and does not process, collect, or hold any payments between Customers and Vendors. All payment arrangements are made directly and independently between the two parties, outside the Platform.

As a result, Evenzoo does not currently issue refunds, since no payments pass through our systems. Any payment disputes at this stage are a matter between the Customer and Vendor directly.

2. Booking Cancellations (Non-Payment)

You may cancel a booking request at any point before it is confirmed by the vendor, free of charge, directly from the My Bookings section of your account.

If a vendor has already confirmed a booking and either party needs to cancel, we recommend communicating directly through the in-app chat to reach a fair resolution regarding any informal arrangements made outside the Platform.

3. What Happens When Payments Launch

Once Evenzoo introduces in-platform payments via Razorpay, this policy will be replaced with a detailed refund schedule covering cancellation timeframes, vendor no-shows, and dispute resolution. We will notify all users in advance of this change.
