1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. Evenzoo also uses browser local storage, a similar technology, to keep you logged in between visits.

2. What We Use

Evenzoo only uses strictly necessary storage. We do not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or sell any data to advertisers.

A note on our consent banner: Because we have no optional or non-essential storage today, both "Accept" and "Essential Only" result in identical functionality. We ask for your choice anyway, and log it, so that if we ever introduce optional features like analytics in the future, your original preference is already on record and will be respected.

Name Purpose Duration
access_token Keeps you logged in and authorizes your requests Until logout or expiry (~1 hour)
refresh_token Allows automatic session renewal without re-entering your password Until logout or 7 days
role, user_id, name Used to personalize your navigation and dashboard Until logout
cookie_consent Remembers your cookie preference so we don't ask again every visit Persistent until cleared

3. Third-Party Services

Some third-party services integrated into Evenzoo may set their own cookies when active on a page:

  • Razorpay — sets cookies during the payment checkout process, governed by Razorpay's own cookie policy

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Note that disabling essential storage (like access_token) will log you out and prevent core features like bookings and payments from functioning.
