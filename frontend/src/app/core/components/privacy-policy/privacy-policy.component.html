1. Introduction

Evenzoo ("we", "us", "our") operates a marketplace platform connecting customers with event vendors (photographers, caterers, decorators, and similar service providers) in India. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

By registering for or using Evenzoo, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Name, email address, and phone number when you register
  • Profile photo, if you choose to upload one
  • For vendors: business name, description, address, service pricing, photos of your work, and business documents (GSTIN/PAN) if submitted for verification
  • Booking details: event date, location, guest count, and special requests
  • Messages sent through our in-app chat feature
  • Reviews and ratings you submit

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device information (browser type, operating system) via standard web request headers
  • IP address, used for session/device tracking and security purposes
  • Login session data, including active device sessions you can view and revoke in your account settings

2.3 Payment information

Phase One Notice: Evenzoo does not currently process or collect payments through the Platform. Any payment arrangement between a Customer and Vendor takes place directly between them, outside Evenzoo, and we do not collect or store any related payment data at this stage.

Once Evenzoo introduces in-platform payments, this section will be updated to reflect our use of Razorpay, a licensed and RBI-regulated payment gateway, and this Privacy Policy will be revised accordingly.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To connect customers with vendors and facilitate bookings
  • To process payments and calculate commission splits between Evenzoo and vendors
  • To send booking confirmations, status updates, and payment receipts
  • To verify your identity via OTP during registration and login
  • To display vendor profiles, ratings, and reviews to help customers make informed decisions
  • To respond to support requests and resolve disputes
  • To improve our platform and detect fraudulent or abusive activity

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We share information only in these circumstances:

  • Between customers and vendors: When you make a booking, relevant details (name, contact information, event details) are shared with the vendor you're booking, and vice versa, so the service can actually be delivered.
  • Payment processing: Transaction details are shared with Razorpay to process your payment securely.
  • SMS/OTP delivery: Your phone number is shared with our SMS provider (MSG91) solely to deliver one-time passwords for verification.
  • Cloud storage: Photos you upload are stored via Cloudinary, our image hosting provider.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or government request.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • All data transmitted between your device and our servers uses HTTPS encryption
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt hashing and are never stored in plain text
  • Access to sensitive data is restricted to authorized personnel only

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active, and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal, accounting, or reporting obligations (such as transaction records required under Indian tax law).

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information via your profile settings
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • View and revoke active login sessions from your account settings
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address listed below.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies and browser local storage to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. See our Cookie Policy for details.

9. Children's Privacy

Evenzoo is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or an in-app notice. Continued use of Evenzoo after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
